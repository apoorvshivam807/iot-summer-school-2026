# Assignment Questions (100 Marks)

Q1. What is a drone (UAV)? Explain its definition, history, and evolution.  [5 Marks]

Q2. List at least five real-world applications of drones.  [5 Marks]

Q3. Differentiate between: (a) UAV and Drone, (b) Fixed-wing and Multirotor drones, (c) Quadcopter and Hexacopter.  [10 Marks]

Q4. Explain how a quadcopter flies. Include lift, thrust, gravity, drag, propeller rotation, and clockwise (CW) / counter-clockwise (CCW) motors. Draw a neat diagram.  [10 Marks]

Q5. Explain how a drone performs take-off, landing, hovering, move forward, move backward, move left, move right, and rotate (yaw). Use suitable diagrams.  [10 Marks]

Q6. Draw a labeled block diagram of a drone and explain the function of each component: flight controller, ESC, brushless motors, propellers, battery, power distribution board, GPS module, radio receiver, frame, telemetry module.  [10 Marks]

Q7. Explain the role of the following components: flight controller, ESC, BLDC motor, propeller, Li-Po battery, GPS, radio transmitter, frame, battery eliminator circuit (BEC), telemetry module.  [10 Marks]

Q8. Explain the working principle and application of IMU, accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, barometer, GPS, optical flow sensor, ultrasonic sensor, LiDAR, and camera. For each sensor mention purpose, working principle, and application.  [10 Marks]

Q9. Compare: (a) GPS vs Optical Flow, (b) LiDAR vs Ultrasonic, (c) Gyroscope vs Accelerometer.  [5 Marks]

Q10. Explain the concept of 6 Degrees of Freedom (6DOF). Include translation, rotation, X-axis, Y-axis, Z-axis. Draw suitable diagrams.  [5 Marks]

Q11. Explain roll, pitch, yaw, surge, sway, and heave with examples of each movement in a drone.  [10 Marks]

Q12. Explain the complete signal flow in a drone: Pilot Input to Radio Transmitter to Receiver to Flight Controller to ESC to BLDC Motors to Propellers to Drone Movement. Illustrate with a block diagram.  [5 Marks]

Q13. Why is PID control important in drones? Explain the proportional, integral, and derivative terms.  [5 Marks]

Q14. Bonus — Research Questions. Answer any TWO of the following:  [10 Marks]
* Compare DJI, ArduPilot, and PX4 flight controllers.
* Explain obstacle avoidance in drones.
* How do drones return to home automatically?
* Explain drone swarm technology.
* What is autonomous navigation?
* Explain computer vision in drones.
* Discuss future trends in drone technology.

Q15. Practical Activity — Choose ONE:  [10 Marks]
* Identify and label every component of a real drone or a drone image.
* Create a 3D CAD model or hand sketch of a quadcopter.
* Use a drone simulator and document take-off, hover, roll, pitch, yaw, and landing with screenshots/video and observations.
